Introduction

What is CyberCafe Xpress?

CyberCafe Xpress is a small-to-medium cyber café proposal focused on reliable network connectivity, straightforward session management, and a comfortable environment for students, gamers, and productivity users.

Target: Students & community
Objectives
  • Provide reliable PC and internet access
  • Support printing and basic productivity needs
  • Ensure clear session management that is easy to audit
Value proposition
  • A premium yet practical setup
  • Wired LAN for performance, WiFi for mobile users
  • A network blueprint that is easy to troubleshoot
Location
Shop lot 15’ × 25’

A straightforward layout that supports crowd control and clean cable management.

Business model
Pay-per-hour + packages

Flexible rates for study, gaming, and printing services.

Operations
Session billing system

Reduces manual errors and simplifies log and revenue tracking.

Problem statement

Many cyber cafés struggle with inconsistent networks—high latency, congestion during peak hours, and difficult troubleshooting. This proposal prioritizes the most critical requirement: stability.

Peak-hour congestion
WiFi alone is not sufficient for 10–20 workstations running concurrently.
Unplanned scaling
As more PCs are added, the network becomes harder to manage and downtime increases.
Security risks and misuse
Without segmentation, a single issue can impact all devices.
