Introduction

Overview of CyberCafe Xpress

CyberCafe Xpress is a small-to-medium cyber café concept that prioritises reliable connectivity, straightforward session management, and a comfortable environment for students, gamers, and productivity-focused users.

Target: Students & community
Objectives
  • Provide reliable PC and internet access
  • Support printing and essential productivity needs
  • Maintain session management that is clear and auditable
Value proposition
  • A premium experience with a practical deployment approach
  • Wired LAN for performance, Wi‑Fi for mobile users
  • A network blueprint designed for straightforward troubleshooting
Location
Shop lot 15’ × 25’

A practical layout that supports customer flow and tidy cable management.

Business model
Pay-per-hour + packages

Flexible rates for study, gaming, and printing services.

Operations
Session billing system

Reduces manual errors and simplifies usage logs and revenue tracking.

Problem statement

Many cyber cafés struggle with inconsistent connectivity—high latency, congestion during peak hours, and time-consuming troubleshooting. This proposal prioritises the most critical requirement: stability.

Peak-hour congestion
Wi‑Fi alone is not sufficient for 10–20 workstations running concurrently.
Unplanned scaling
As more PCs are added, management complexity increases and downtime risk rises.
Security risks and misuse
Without segmentation, a single issue can affect the entire environment.
